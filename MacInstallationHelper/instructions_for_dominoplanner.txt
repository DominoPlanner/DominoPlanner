================================================================================
RUNNING DOMINOPLANNER ON MACOS (NON-DEVELOPER GUIDE)
================================================================================

Because DominoPlanner is a self-contained, independent application built without 
a paid Apple Developer certificate, modern versions of macOS will block it by 
default when you try to open it for the first time.

This security feature is standard Apple behavior to prevent running unverified 
code from the internet, but you can easily bypass it manually.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY DOESN'T IT OPEN RIGHT AWAY?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you double-click an app that isn't signed by an officially registered 
Apple Developer, macOS quarantines the app and prevents it from executing to 
protect your system. You might see a warning saying that "the app cannot be 
opened because the developer cannot be verified" or that "it is damaged."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO RUN IT ANYWAY (STEP-BY-STEP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You only need to do this once on your Mac:

  • Right-click (or hold the Control key and click) on "DominoPlanner.app".
  • Select "Open" from the top of the context menu.
  • A warning dialog will pop up stating that macOS cannot verify the 
    developer. Click the "Open" button anyway.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ALTERNATIVE METHOD (VIA SYSTEM SETTINGS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If the right-click method doesn't give you the option to open it:

  1. Try to open the app normally once (it will be blocked).
  2. Open "System Settings" -> "Privacy & Security".
  3. Scroll down to the security section — you will see a message saying 
     "DominoPlanner.app was blocked from use because it is not from an 
     identified developer."
  4. Click "Open Anyway" and confirm your Mac password.

After doing this once, the app is fully trusted on your system and will open 
normally with a standard double-click from then on!
